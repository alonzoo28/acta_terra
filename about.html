About ActaTerra

Our mission and the team behind the movement.

Our Mission

ActaTerra was founded on the belief that climate action should not be complicated. By translating the complex targets of SDG 13: Climate Action into simple, daily habits, we empower individuals to reduce their environmental footprint—one task at a time.

Why SDG 13?

Climate change is a global emergency that goes beyond borders. Our platform focuses on major environmental pillars to help users build a more resilient and sustainable future.
